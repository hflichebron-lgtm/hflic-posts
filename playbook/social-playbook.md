# HFLIC Social Playbook

Updated: November 7, 2026. Covers November 8 to December 7, 2026.

## What's working for HFLIC

Data: 4 Instagram carousels from Nov 4 to 6 (Windsor). The Nov 7 Reel was not in the data yet. No TikTok data (not live, no connector).

- Rule of 72 Lesson, 9:58 PM: reach 110, 15 likes.
- 5 majors Top 5, 2:31 PM: reach 99, 12 likes.
- 18th birthday checklist Lesson, 9:50 AM: reach 79, 7 likes, 1 comment.
- 5 niche finance jobs Lesson, 10:01 AM: reach 76, 9 likes.
- Average reach 91, like rate about 12%. Tiny sample, so treat as early signals.
- Afternoon and evening posts averaged 105 reach vs 78 for mid-morning.
- One surprising number plus a simple trick (Rule of 72) beat career lists.
- Biggest gap: zero saves and zero shares on every post. Every post needs a save reason and a send reason.

## Instagram rules this month

- Top signals are still watch time, likes and sends. Sends matter most for reaching new people.
- Since Oct 21, Reels are ranked as sequences, and about 45% of recommended Reels are under a day old. Post timely topics fast and build numbered series.
- Originality rule: reposted or lightly edited content loses recommendations. Keep every graphic and script our own.
- Max 5 hashtags. Put keywords in the caption and on slide 1 for search. No hashtags in comments.
- Turn on the post AI label for Reels with realistic AI voices. Labeling does not cut reach. The "AI-generated profile" label is only for accounts featuring an AI person, so HFLIC doesn't need it.
- Keep links in bio. In-post links need a paid Meta One plan.
- Some states now hide like counts for teens. Optimize for saves and sends.
- Times: Thursday 9 AM is strongest, with a lift around 6 PM. Friday and weekends are weakest. Test one daily post at about 6 PM CT.

## TikTok rules this month

- Hook in the first 2 seconds, with on-screen text matching the spoken hook.
- Our 35 to 60 second Reels are fine. Under 25 seconds tests well for single facts.
- Slideshows: 5 to 7 slides, a bold question or number on slide 1, short text per slide. Photo posts get more likes and comments but fewer shares, so end with a "send this to" slide.
- Audio: add a quiet trending sound to slideshows. In Reels, keep music under the voices.
- Realistic AI voices need TikTok's AI-generated label (generic text-to-speech does not). Labeling does not lower distribution; unlabeled posts can get a forced label or less reach.
- Search: keyword-rich captions, say the keyword out loud, 3 to 5 hashtags.
- Finance: no guaranteed returns, no get-rich-quick claims, no buy or sell calls. Keep "Education only, not financial advice."
- Times: Sunday 9 AM and weekday evenings (6 to 11 PM) are strongest. Wednesday is weakest.

## Hooks and formats to use

- "Major cheat code in life:" (trending now) for one money rule.
- Christmas countdown: "X days until Christmas" plus a money fact, weekly.
- "Money can't buy happiness, but..." with a student treat and its real cost.
- "Teeny tiny message" zoom reveal for one surprising number.
- Series: "Black Friday Math" Parts 1 to 3, and "Explain like I'm 16" for CPI, jobs and Fed news.

Example hooks:

1. "Major cheat code in life: the Rule of 72."
2. "Prices went up 3.7% in a year. Here's what that does to your $20."
3. "Shoppers will spend $12.9 billion online on Black Friday. Don't pay credit card interest on it for months."
4. "Teens spend about $2,350 a year. Here's where it goes."
5. "48 days until Christmas. Save $5 a day starting now and you'll have $240."

## Avoid

- Posts with no reason to save or send.
- Career lists without one shocking number on slide 1.
- Only posting mid-morning.
- Unlabeled realistic AI voices.
- More than 5 hashtags.
- Political takes on the Fed or elections. Explain, don't pick sides.
