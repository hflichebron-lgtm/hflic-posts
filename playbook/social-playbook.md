- Average reach 91, like rate about 12%. Small sample, so treat as early signals only.

- Biggest gap: zero saves and zero shares on every post. Sends and saves drive reach to non-followers, so every post this month needs a save reason and a send reason.

## Instagram rules this month

- Top ranking signals are still watch time, likes and sends (Mosseri). Sends matter most for reaching new people.
- Since Oct 21, Reels are ranked as sequences, and about 45% of recommended Reels are under a day old. Post timely topics fast, and build numbered series so one Reel leads to the next.
- Originality rule (since Apr 30): reposted or lightly edited content loses recommendations. Keep every graphic and script our own.
- Hashtags: max 5 per post. Put keywords in the caption and on the first slide (Instagram search reads them). No hashtags in comments.
- AI labels: HFLIC does not need the "AI-generated profile" label (that is for accounts featuring an AI person). Turn on the post-level AI label for any Reel using realistic AI voices. Labeling does not cut reach.
- Links: keep links in bio. In-post links are limited to paid Meta One plans.
- Teens: some states now hide like counts for teen accounts, and California may limit teen feeds from Jan 1, 2027. Optimize for saves and sends, not likes.
- Times: 9 AM Thursday is the strongest general slot, plus a lift around 6 PM; Friday and weekends are weakest. Test moving one daily post to about 6 PM CT.

## TikTok rules this month

- Hook in the first 2 seconds, with on-screen text that repeats the spoken hook.
- Length: our 35 to 60 second Reels are fine. Under 25 seconds tests well for simple facts, so cut a short version when the script allows.
- Slideshows (photo mode): 5 to 7 slides, bold question or number on slide 1, short text per slide, alt text on images. Photo posts get more likes and comments than video but fewer shares, so end with a "send this to" slide.
- Audio: add a low-volume trending sound to slideshows. For Reels with voices, keep music under the voices.
- AI voices: generic text-to-speech needs no label, but realistic AI voices do. Turn on the AI-generated content label for our two-voice Reels. TikTok says labeling does not lower distribution; unlabeled content can get a forced label or less reach.
- Search: write keyword-rich captions (TikTok allows 2,200 characters), say the keyword out loud, use 3 to 5 hashtags.
- Finance content: no guaranteed returns, no get-rich-quick claims, no specific buy or sell calls. Keep "Education only, not financial advice."
- Times: Sunday 9 AM and weekday 6 to 11 PM are strongest; Wednesday is weakest.

## Hooks and formats to use

- "Major cheat code in life:" (trending Instagram format) for one money rule per Reel.
- Christmas countdown: "X days until Christmas" plus a weekly money fact. Good series for sequence ranking.
- "Money can't buy happiness, but..." with a small student-budget treat and a cost breakdown.
- "Teeny tiny message" zoom reveal for one surprising number.
- Series: "Black Friday Math" (Parts 1 to 3) and "Explain like I'm 16" for Fed, CPI and jobs reports.

Example hooks for HFLIC:

1. "Major cheat code in life: the Rule of 72."
2. "Prices went up 3.7% in a year. Here's what that does to your $20."
3. "Black Friday shoppers will spend $12.9 billion online in one day. Don't be the one paying credit card interest on it for months."
4. "Teens spend about $2,350 a year. Here's where it goes."
5. "48 days until Christmas. Save $5 a day starting now and you'll have $240."

## Avoid

- Posts with no reason to save or send. Add a checklist, a "save for later" slide, or "send this to a friend who...".
- Long lists of careers without one shocking number on slide 1.
- Posting mid-morning only. Test afternoon and evening.
- Unlabeled realistic AI voices on Reels.
- More than 5 hashtags, or hashtags in comments.
- Political takes on the Fed or the midterms. Explain, don't pick sides.
- Unconfirmed dates (earnings, FAFSA). Check the official page the day before.
