Minimum wages go up in many states on Jan. 1, 2027. These are the four highest statewide rates:

1. Washington: $17.73 an hour (up 3.5%)
2. Connecticut: $17.48
3. California: $17.40
4. Rhode Island: $17.00

At 40 hours a week, Washington's rate works out to $709.20 before taxes. Texas still uses the federal minimum of $7.25, or $290 for 40 hours. Statewide rates only; some cities and regions set their own.

Data: Washington L&I (Sept. 30, 2026); Office of Gov. Lamont (Aug. 5, 2026); California DIR (Aug. 13, 2026); Office of Gov. McKee (Aug. 4, 2025); Texas Workforce Commission.
