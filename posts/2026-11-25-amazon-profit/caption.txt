Amazon brought in $201.9 billion in sales from July through September 2026, up 12% from a year earlier. Most came from its stores: $112.9 billion in North America and $43.1 billion internationally.

Its cloud business, Amazon Web Services (AWS), had $46.0 billion in sales, up 39%. AWS also earned $17.8 billion of Amazon's $28.1 billion in operating income. That's about 63% of the profit from less than a quarter of the sales.

Amazon's operating margin hit 13.9%, its highest ever, and it expects about $220 billion in cash capital spending in 2026.
