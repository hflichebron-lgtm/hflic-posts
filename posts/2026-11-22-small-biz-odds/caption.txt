There are 36.2 million small businesses in the U.S., and 82.3% of them have no paid employees.

How long do new ones last? From 1994 to 2022, an average of 67.7% of new employer establishments survived at least two years. About half (49.2%) made it five years, and 33.9% made it ten. In 2023, 1.3 million business locations opened and about 1.2 million closed for good.
