Buy now, pay later (BNPL) splits a purchase into smaller payments. The most common version is pay-in-four: four equal payments over six weeks, with no extra interest or fees if you pay on time. Adobe expects $21.3 billion in BNPL spending online this holiday season.

In 2025, 16% of U.S. adults used it, including 22% of adults 18 to 29. And 29% of users said it was the only way they could afford the purchase.

The catch: 26% of BNPL users paid late at least once, and 32% of users 18 to 29 did. 17% of all users were charged extra for paying late.

Data: Federal Reserve, Economic Well-Being of U.S. Households in 2025 (May 2026) and Consumer & Community Context (Aug. 2026); Adobe holiday forecast (Sept. 28, 2026).
