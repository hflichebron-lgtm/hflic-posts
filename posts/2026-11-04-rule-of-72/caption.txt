Quick math trick: divide 72 by your yearly return to see how many years it takes your money to double.

At 9%, that's about 8 years. Start at 18 and a single $1,000 could grow to around $16,000 by age 50 without adding another dollar. Returns aren't guaranteed, but time is the biggest advantage you have right now.
