U.S. holiday retail sales are forecast to top $1 trillion this year: $1.013 trillion for November and December, up 4.2% from $972 billion in 2025 (National Retail Federation). About half of that growth is expected to come from higher prices (AP).

NRF's survey of 8,094 adults: 43% started shopping before November, 64% plan to do most of it over Thanksgiving weekend, and gift cards top wish lists (47%), followed by clothing (44%). Among the 56% who say the economy will affect their plans, 43% will shop sales more often.

Your move: set a gift budget before Black Friday and stick to it.

Data: NRF holiday forecast (Oct. 15, 2026) and survey (Oct. 20, 2026); Associated Press.
