Credit cards that charged interest averaged a 22.36% APR in the third quarter of 2026 (Federal Reserve). Here's what that means on a $1,000 balance.

22.36% a year is about 1.86% a month, so the first month costs about $18.63 in interest. Pay $35 a month and it takes 42 months to pay off, with about $441 in interest. Pay $100 a month and it's gone in 12 months, with about $117 in interest (example, no new purchases).

The way to pay $0: most cards have a grace period, so if you pay the full statement balance by the due date, you generally aren't charged interest on purchases.

Data: Federal Reserve G.19 Consumer Credit (Nov. 6, 2026); CFPB.
