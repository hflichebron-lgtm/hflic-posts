Netflix kept about 35 cents of every dollar as operating profit last quarter. Revenue was $12.87 billion, up about 12% from a year earlier, with a 34.6% operating margin (what's left after costs like content, marketing and tech, before interest and taxes).

Ads are growing but still small: Netflix now expects about $2.7 billion in ad revenue for 2026, down from its earlier goal of about $3 billion, out of about $51.1 billion in total revenue it forecasts for the year. That's roughly 5%.

The model: members pay every month, and a show costs the same to make whether 10 million or 100 million people watch it. More viewers spread that cost thinner.

Data: Netflix Q3 2026 results (Oct. 20, 2026), as reported by TheDesk and Advanced Television.
