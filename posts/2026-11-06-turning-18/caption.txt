16 or 17? You've got one more year left. It's time to prepare.

Turning 18 is when your credit story can officially start. Here's the checklist:

Before 18: ask a parent to add you as an authorized user on a card they pay on time, open a checking and savings account, and get a job (earned income is what lets you open a Roth IRA).

At 18: pull your free credit reports every week at AnnualCreditReport.com, get a student or secured card (under 21 you need your own income or a cosigner), and open a Roth IRA. The 2026 limit is $7,500, up to what you earn.

Why the rush? $100 a month at 7% from age 18 grows to about $438,600 by 65. Start at 28 and it's about $209,700. Ten extra years, $12,000 more put in, about $229,000 more at the end.
