Roblox's revenue rose 16% last quarter while its bookings fell 12%. Both numbers are real.

Bookings are what players spent in the quarter: $1.697 billion, down from $1.922 billion, mainly because fewer people paid (about 1.8 million daily paying users, down from about 2.3 million). Revenue was $1.572 billion, up from $1.360 billion. Roblox counts most spending as revenue slowly, over the 27-month average life of a paying player, so revenue can keep rising for a while after spending slows.

Also: about 138 million people played on an average day, and Roblox paid game creators $403 million.

Data: Roblox Form 10-Q for the quarter ended Sept. 30, 2026 (SEC).
