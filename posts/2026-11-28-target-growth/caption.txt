Target's sales grew 7.1% in its third quarter of 2026, with comparable sales up 5.6% and traffic up 5.0%.

The fastest growth came from its Fun101 toys and fun category (nearly 27%, with record Pokémon sales) and from non-merchandise sales like its Roundel ad business, Target Circle Card and Circle 360 memberships (nearly 20%). Beauty grew more than 9% and food and beverage nearly 7%. Same-day delivery now reaches 80% of the U.S. population, and same-day services drive two-thirds of Target's digital business.

For the holidays, Target added more than 8,000 new gifts, and most cost $20 or less.

Data: Target Q3 2026 earnings release (Nov. 18, 2026).
