Seniors: the 2027 to 28 FAFSA is already open. It launched in late September 2026, the earliest launch in the program's history.

The FAFSA is the free form for federal grants, work-study and student loans, and many states and colleges use it for their own aid. It uses your family's 2025 tax information. The federal deadline is June 30, 2028, but colleges usually have the earliest deadlines, states may set their own, and some aid is limited. The Education Department's advice: file as early as you can.

To start, make a StudentAid.gov account. Parents or other contributors need one too, and you can now invite them by text or email. The department says the form takes about 15 minutes on average.
