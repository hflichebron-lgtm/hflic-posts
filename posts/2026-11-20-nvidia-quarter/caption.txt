Nvidia brought in $115.8 billion in revenue in its latest quarter (ended Oct. 25, 2026), up 103% from a year earlier and 20% from the quarter before.

About 93% of that, $107.6 billion, came from its Data Center business, which sells the chips and systems that run AI. Nvidia kept 74.8% of revenue after the direct cost of making its products (gross margin), and its net income was $65.3 billion, about 56 cents of every dollar of sales.

Its outlook for next quarter is about $130 billion, plus or minus 3%. That's a company forecast, not a guarantee.

Data: Nvidia Q3 fiscal 2027 results (Nov. 17, 2026).
