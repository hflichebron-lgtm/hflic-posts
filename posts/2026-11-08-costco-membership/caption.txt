Costco sold $297.2 billion of stuff in fiscal 2026 and kept about 3 cents of profit per dollar: $9.2 billion in net income on $303.2 billion of revenue.

The bigger story is the membership card. Membership fees brought in $5.9 billion, equal to about half of Costco's $11.7 billion operating profit (what it earns from running the business, before interest and taxes). And 92.3% of U.S. and Canada members renew, so that money comes back every year. That's called recurring revenue.

Data: Costco fiscal 2026 results, 52 weeks ended Aug. 30, 2026.
