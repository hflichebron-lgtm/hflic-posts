Shopping online for Black Friday? The card you pay with changes how much fraud can cost you.

Credit cards: by law, the most you'll owe for unauthorized charges is $50, and if only your card number was stolen (not the card), you generally owe nothing (CFPB).

Debit cards: it depends on how fast you report. Within 2 business days of learning your card was lost or stolen, you're liable for at most $50. Within 60 days of your statement, up to $500. After that, you could be liable for everything taken. Many card networks add their own $0-liability policies, but debit fraud still takes your own cash out of your account while it's sorted out.

Data: CFPB; Federal Reserve Consumer Compliance Outlook (Oct. 2025) on Regulation E.
