Moving out after graduation? The median one-bedroom rent in the U.S. was $1,215 a month in October 2026 (Apartment List).

The usual rule: housing is considered affordable if it costs no more than 30% of your income. That means earning about 40 times your monthly rent each year. For $1,215, that's about $48,600 a year before taxes, or about $23.37 an hour working full time.

Compare that with a first job: a full-time cashier at the $15.81 median earns about $32,900 a year, so 30% is about $822 a month. A roommate is one way to close the gap. Rents vary a lot by city, so run the math on real listings near you.

Data: Apartment List national rent report (October 2026, via CRE Daily); Congressional Research Service (Aug. 2026); BLS cashier pay (May 2025).
