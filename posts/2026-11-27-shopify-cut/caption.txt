From July through September 2026, shoppers spent $119.1 billion at stores built on Shopify (its gross merchandise volume), up 29% from a year earlier.

Shopify's revenue was $3.74 billion, up 32%. Rough HFLIC math: that's about 3 cents of every dollar sold. 78% came from merchant solutions, mainly payment processing fees from Shopify Payments. The rest, $836 million, came from subscriptions like plans, apps and themes. Operating income rose to $568 million from $343 million.

Data: Shopify Form 10-Q for the quarter ended Sept. 30, 2026 (SEC EDGAR).
