New inflation numbers come out today at 8:30 AM ET. Here's how to read them.

The latest reading (September 2026) shows prices up 3.7% in a year. Core inflation, which leaves out food and energy, was 2.6%. The gap comes from energy: gasoline rose 34.2% in a year. A tank that cost $40 a year ago costs about $53.68 now (example).

The money lesson: if your pay or your savings grow slower than prices, you can buy less than before. A 3% raise with 3.7% inflation is a small pay cut in real terms.

Data: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, CPI for September 2026 (released Oct. 14, 2026).
