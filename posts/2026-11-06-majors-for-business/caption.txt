Not into a business major? You can still end up running the business. Here are 5 majors that work really well in the business world (according to Claude).

1. Computer science: product management, tech consulting, business analytics
2. Industrial engineering: operations and supply chains
3. Physics: modeling hard problems, which consulting and quant teams love
4. Mathematics: data, pricing, and analytics
5. Psychology: marketing, HR, and user research

Mid-career median pay (ages 35 to 45): computer science $120,000, physics $105,000, industrial engineering and math $100,000 each, compared with $87,000 for all majors. Psychology comes in at $72,000, so pair it with a business minor or data skills.

Pay data: Federal Reserve Bank of New York, The Labor Market for Recent College Graduates. Rankings are according to Claude.
