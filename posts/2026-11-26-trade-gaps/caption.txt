A trade deficit with a country means the U.S. imports more goods from it than it exports there. In September 2026, the four biggest gaps were:

1. Mexico: $28.7 billion
2. Vietnam: $25.3 billion
3. Taiwan: $21.9 billion
4. China: $17.9 billion

Imports from Taiwan jumped $4.2 billion in September to $27.3 billion. Counting goods and services together, the overall U.S. trade deficit was $105.0 billion for the month.

Data: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis and U.S. Census Bureau, September 2026 trade release (Nov. 4, 2026). Goods only for country figures.
