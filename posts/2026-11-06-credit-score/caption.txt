Your credit score isn't a mystery. FICO scores come down to 5 factors, and two of them (payment history and amounts owed) make up 65% of the score.

Pay on time, every time. Keep your balance low compared to your limit. On a $1,000 limit, carrying $100 looks a lot better than carrying $300.
