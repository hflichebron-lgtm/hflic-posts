An index fund lets you own a slice of the whole market in one purchase. An S&P 500 index fund holds about 500 of the biggest US companies.

The part most people miss: fees. $10,000 growing for 30 years at 7% before fees ends up around $75,500 with a 0.03% fee, but only about $57,400 with a 1% fee. That's roughly $18,000 lost to fees.
