Got your first paycheck and it's less than you thought? That's the difference between gross pay and net pay.

Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%) come out of every check, plus federal income tax based on your pay and your W-4. Good news if you work in Texas: there's no state income tax.

If you work part-time, file a tax return. You might get some of that federal tax back.
