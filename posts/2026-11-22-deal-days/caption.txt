Black Friday is Nov. 27 and Cyber Monday is Nov. 30. Adobe's 2026 forecast says the deepest online discounts depend on what you're buying.

Electronics are expected to peak at 30% off listed price, with the best deals on Cyber Monday. Toys: 29%, best on Black Friday. TVs, computers and apparel: about 23%. In December, Adobe expects deals of only up to 10% to 15%.

These are forecasts, not promises. Make a list, set a budget and compare prices before you buy. Buy now, pay later is still borrowing, and Adobe expects $21.3 billion of it this season.

Data: Adobe Digital Insights 2026 holiday forecast (Sept. 28, 2026).
