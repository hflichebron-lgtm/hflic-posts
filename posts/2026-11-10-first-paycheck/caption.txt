Your first paycheck will be smaller than your hours times your pay. Here's why, using a $300 example (20 hours at $15 an hour).

Social Security takes 6.2% ($18.60) and Medicare takes 1.45% ($4.35), so you keep $277.05 before any income tax. Those two taxes don't come back as a refund.

Federal income tax is different. The 2026 standard deduction is $16,100, so if your only income is from a job and you earn less than that this year, you likely owe no federal income tax. If your employer withheld some anyway, file a tax return to get it back.

Data: Social Security Administration 2026 tax rates; IRS tax year 2026 inflation adjustments (Oct. 9, 2025).
