AAA expects 81.5 million Americans to travel at least 50 miles for Thanksgiving (Nov. 24 to 30), down 0.1% from last year's 81.6 million. It's the first dip since 2023.

About 72.9 million are driving and 5.96 million are flying. Domestic roundtrip airfare averages about $800, up 17%. Gas averaged $3.64 a gallon nationally the week of Nov. 23, up 58 cents from a year earlier. In Texas, it was $3.03.

Driving back this weekend? INRIX data via AAA says to leave before 11 AM on Saturday or Sunday. Sunday's worst traffic is from noon to 8 PM.

Data: AAA Thanksgiving forecast (Nov. 16, 2026); U.S. Energy Information Administration (week of Nov. 23, 2026).
