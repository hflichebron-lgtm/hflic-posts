Spotify had 788 million users last quarter, but only 306 million (about 39%) pay for Premium. Those subscribers brought in €4.49 billion of its €4.97 billion in revenue, about 90%.

Per person, that's about €14.66 per Premium subscriber over three months, versus about €0.97 per free, ad-supported user. And Spotify keeps only about 33% of revenue after direct costs, which are mostly royalties for the music (34.5% on Premium, 18.7% on ads).

Operating income was €672 million, up 15% from a year earlier.

Data: Spotify Q3 2026 results (Oct. 22, 2026, SEC Form 6-K); Spotify 2025 Form 20-F.
