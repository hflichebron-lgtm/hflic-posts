A 401(k) is a retirement account you get through work. For 2027, the IRS raised the employee limit to $25,500, up from $24,500 in 2026.

Some employers add a match. Example (hypothetical): you earn $40,000 and your employer matches 50% of what you put in, up to 6% of pay. Put in $2,400 and they add $1,200. In mid-2026, employers in plans Fidelity tracks contributed 4.8% of pay on average.

Check the vesting schedule. Your own contributions are always 100% yours, but the employer's money can take up to 6 years to fully vest.

Data: IRS IR-2026-136 (Nov. 12, 2026); IRS Retirement topics: Vesting; Fidelity Q2 2026 Retirement Analysis.
