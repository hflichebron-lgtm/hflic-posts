Where does your $10 at Chipotle go? Using Chipotle's own Q3 2026 costs as a share of revenue:

About $2.95 goes to food, beverages and packaging (29.5%), $2.57 to workers (25.7%), and $2.14 to restaurant rent and other operating costs (21.4%). That leaves about $2.33 at the restaurant level (a 23.3% margin). After corporate costs, depreciation and opening new restaurants, about $1.37 is operating profit (13.7%).

Small slices, huge volume: Chipotle had $3.3 billion in revenue in those three months, from 4,267 company-owned restaurants.

Data: Chipotle Q3 2026 results (Oct. 28, 2026), SEC filing. Restaurant-level margin is a non-GAAP measure.
