Scammers go where young people scroll. In 2025, 40% of money-loss scam reports from 18 to 29-year-olds started on social media, the highest of any age group (FTC). Reported losses to social media scams hit $2.1 billion, about eight times 2020's level.

The 3 to know: fake shop ads (the most reported scam that starts on social media), fake rentals (18 to 29-year-olds were 3 times as likely as other adults to report losing money, median loss $1,000) and fake jobs (almost $300 million lost to job scams in the first half of 2025).

The rule that covers all three: never pay first for something you can't verify. Tour a rental before you pay, and real employers never make you pay to get hired.

Data: FTC Data Spotlights (April 2026 and December 2025) and FTC job scam data via Fortune (November 2025).
