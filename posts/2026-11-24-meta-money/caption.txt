Instagram is free to use. Meta, the company behind it, brought in $64.6 billion in revenue from July through September 2026, up 26% from a year earlier.

$63.1 billion of that came from ads. Meta showed 10% more ads and charged 14% more per ad. On average, 3.64 billion people used its apps every day in September. Rough HFLIC math: that's about $17.75 in revenue per daily user for the quarter.

Meta also spent $34.9 billion on buildings and equipment in the quarter, and its Reality Labs unit (headsets and smart glasses) lost $4.1 billion.
