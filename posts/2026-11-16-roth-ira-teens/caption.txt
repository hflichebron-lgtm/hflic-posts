Have a job? You can open a Roth IRA at any age, as long as you have taxable compensation like pay from work (IRS). If you're under 18, a parent usually opens it as a custodial account.

How it works: you put in money you've already paid tax on, and qualified withdrawals in retirement come out tax-free, growth included. The 2026 limit is $7,500, or what you earned that year if it's less.

Example: $2,000 a year at ages 16, 17 and 18, then nothing else. At an assumed 7% a year, that $6,000 grows to about $154,600 by 65. The 7% is an assumption, not a promise, but time is on your side.

Data: IRS (Traditional and Roth IRAs; Topic 309); 2026 limit from IRS Notice 2025-67 via CPA Practice Advisor.
