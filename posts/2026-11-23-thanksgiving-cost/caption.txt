A classic Thanksgiving dinner for 10 costs $58.63 this year, about $5.86 a person, according to the American Farm Bureau Federation's 41st annual survey. That's up 6% from 2025 but still more than 8% below the 2022 record.

The turkey drove it: a 16-pound frozen bird averages $23.68, up 10%. Cranberries rose nearly 9%, while the veggie tray fell 3.7% and pie crusts dropped 9 cents. By region: Northeast $61.57, West $58.74, Midwest $56.89, South $54.33.

Hosting? Split the dishes and compare stores. Some volunteer shoppers found turkey for 69 cents a pound.

Data: American Farm Bureau Federation, Thanksgiving dinner cost survey (Nov. 18, 2026).
