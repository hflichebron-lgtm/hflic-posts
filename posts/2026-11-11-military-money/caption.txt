Thank you to everyone who has served. For Veterans Day, here's what military service pays, using official 2026 numbers.

1. Pay from day one: a new E-1 earns $2,407.20 a month in basic pay in 2026 (after a 3.8% raise), about $28,900 a year, plus separate allowances for housing and food.
2. College later: after 36 months of active duty, the Post-9/11 GI Bill covers net in-state tuition and fees at public schools at the 100% level, plus up to $1,000 a year for books. Private schools are capped at $30,908.34 a year for 2026 to 2027.
3. A retirement match: the service puts 1% of your basic pay into a Thrift Savings Plan account, and after 2 years it matches what you put in, up to 5% total.

These benefits come with a multi-year service commitment. Talk to a recruiter and a veteran before you decide.

Data: DFAS 2026 basic pay (via Military.com), VA Post-9/11 GI Bill rates (Aug. 1, 2026), Air Force Blended Retirement System page (reviewed Jan. 30, 2026).
