Seniors: don't let sticker prices scare you off. Average published tuition and fees for 2026-27 (College Board): $4,210 at public two-year colleges, $12,290 at public four-year schools in-state, $32,730 out-of-state and $46,350 at private nonprofits. Housing, food and books are extra.

What families actually pay is often much lower. After grant aid, average net tuition and fees for first-time, full-time students is estimated at about $1,920 at in-state public four-year schools and $17,280 at private nonprofits. At community colleges, grants have covered the average student's tuition and fees since 2009.

Your move: file the FAFSA, then use each college's net price calculator to get an estimate for your family.

Data: College Board, Trends in College Pricing and Student Aid 2026 (via Inside Higher Ed, Nov. 6, 2026).
