Americans reported losing a record $15.9 billion to fraud in 2025, across 3 million reports (FTC). One free move stops someone from opening credit in your name: a credit freeze.

While a freeze is on, nobody can open a new credit account in your name, including you. It's free to place and lift, and it doesn't affect your credit score. To set it up, contact all three bureaus: Equifax, Experian and TransUnion.

Need a card, loan or apartment later? Lift the freeze at the bureau that lender uses, then put it back. Parents can request a free freeze for kids under 16 too.

Data: FTC testimony to Congress (March 25, 2026); FTC Consumer Advice on credit freezes.
