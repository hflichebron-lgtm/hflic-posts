What does each path after high school pay? BLS median weekly earnings for full-time workers 25 and older in 2025:

1. Bachelor's degree: $1,578 a week (unemployment 2.8%)
2. Associate's degree: $1,135 (3.0%)
3. Some college, no degree: $1,062 (3.8%)
4. High school diploma: $966 (4.3%)

Rough HFLIC math (weekly pay x 52): a bachelor's works out to about $82,000 a year, and a diploma to about $50,000. Starting college without finishing adds only $96 a week over a diploma. These are medians, not guarantees, and BLS notes the 2025 averages leave out October 2025.

Data: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Education pays, 2025 (Sept. 2026).
