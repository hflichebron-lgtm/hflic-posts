You can usually stay on a parent's job-based health plan until you turn 26, even if you get married, move out, leave school, have a child or aren't claimed as a tax dependent. On a parent's Marketplace plan, coverage can last through Dec. 31 of the year you turn 26.

Why it matters: in 2025, 26.7 million people (7.9%) had no health insurance for the entire year.

When you lose that coverage, you get a Special Enrollment Period that starts 60 days before and ends 60 days after. Open Enrollment for 2027 HealthCare.gov plans runs Nov. 1, 2026 to Jan. 15, 2027.

Data: HealthCare.gov; U.S. Census Bureau (Sept. 15, 2026); CMS (Oct. 30, 2026).
