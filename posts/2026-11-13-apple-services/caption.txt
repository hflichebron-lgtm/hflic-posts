Apple sold $478.8 billion of stuff in fiscal 2026. The iPhone brought in $254.1 billion, more than half.

The twist is Services (the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, AppleCare, ads and payments). It was 26% of sales but about 40% of gross profit. Apple kept about 76 cents of each Services dollar after direct costs, compared with about 39 cents on hardware. Gross profit comes before costs like research and marketing.

After everything, Apple's net income was $132.2 billion, up 18%.

Data: Apple fiscal 2026 results (year ended Sept. 26, 2026), SEC filing.
