Walmart's revenue was $188.8 billion last quarter, up 5.2% (quarter ended Oct. 31, 2026). Its fastest-growing pieces aren't on the shelves.

Global e-commerce sales grew 17%, membership fee revenue grew 17%, and its advertising business grew 25%, with Walmart Connect (ads shown to shoppers on its website and app) up 36%. Operating income grew 16%, about three times as fast as revenue.

Big retailers now earn from memberships and ads on top of what they sell.

Data: Walmart Q3 fiscal 2027 earnings release (Nov. 19, 2026), SEC filing.
