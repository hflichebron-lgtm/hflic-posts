The average U.S. savings account pays just 0.38% a year (FDIC national rate, October 2026). Some high-yield savings accounts pay about 10 times that, around 3.8% or more right now.

What that means in dollars, for one year: $1,000 earns about $3.80 at the average rate versus about $38 at 3.8%. On $5,000, it's about $19 versus $190.

Before you move money, make sure the account is FDIC- or NCUA-insured and has no monthly fees, and remember that savings rates can change at any time.

Data: FDIC national savings rate via FRED (October 2026); high-yield rates via NerdWallet (November 2026).
