Budgeting doesn't have to be complicated. Try the 50/30/20 rule: 50% of your take-home pay for needs, 30% for wants, 20% for savings.

On $800 a month, that's $160 saved. Do that for a year and you've got $1,920 before any interest. Still living at home? Push that savings number even higher while your bills are low.
