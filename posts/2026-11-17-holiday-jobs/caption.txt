Need holiday cash? These 3 jobs hire a lot of seasonal workers and don't need a degree. Pay is the national median hourly wage from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (May 2025), so half of workers earn more and half earn less.

1. Stocker and order filler: about $17.95 an hour ($37,330 a year median). You move products to shelves or pack orders, and training usually takes a month or less.
2. Retail salesperson: $17.03 an hour. You help customers and run the register, with training that lasts a few days to a few months.
3. Cashier: $15.81 an hour. You ring up purchases and handle payments, with training that may take a few weeks.

All three pay more than double the $7.25 federal minimum wage. Pay varies a lot by state and store, so compare offers.

Pay data: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook (May 2025 wages). Ranked by Claude by median pay.
