U.S. teens say they spend about $2,350 a year, up 6% from last year. That's about $45 a week. Clothing takes about 21% of the average teen's spending, and beauty is now 23% of teen girls' spending, a survey high.

Big athletic brands are losing ground: their share of teen fashion fell from 44% three years ago to 29% now. Brands watch these numbers closely, so know where your money goes. Cutting just $10 a week adds up to $520 a year.

Data: Piper Sandler Taking Stock With Teens survey, fall 2026 (about 9,000 U.S. teens, self-reported), via Retail Dive.
